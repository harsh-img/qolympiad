Our story

Qolympiad exists because young learners deserve assessments that feel fair — not frightening. We combine the structure of a real exam hall with age-appropriate language, extra support where needed, and scoring that explains what happened, not just a single opaque number.

From picture-based tasks for Nursery and primary to board-aware practice for Class 9–10, every sitting follows the same promise: dignified routines, consistent invigilation, and outcomes schools and families can trust.

Mission

Give every child a fair chance to show what they know — without gimmicks, “trick” items, or vague reports that leave parents guessing.

What we do

We design papers, publish schedules, run sittings with trained staff, and deliver results in plain language. Schools can partner for on-campus slots or send students to open centres.

Values

Child safety first, honesty in communication, respect for school syllabi, and transparency in how bands and ranks are calculated.

For schools

  • Bulk registration and cohort reporting options
  • Co-branded or school-branded materials where agreed in writing
  • Aligned papers that complement classroom teaching
  • Dedicated contact for timetabling and venue coordination

For parents & students

  • Two tracks: School (confidence-first) and Scholar (stretch)
  • Clear instructions before the timer starts
  • Results with bands, topics, and next-step hints
  • Help desk for schedule, venue, and account questions
