What this page compares — and what it doesn’t

Clear boundaries below: this isn’t a head-to-head against other olympiad brands or exam boards.

✓ What we do compare here

School Track vs Scholar Track — both inside qolympiad.

  • You can choose the same subjects (Math, Science, English, GK, reasoning — whatever your bundle includes).
  • Paper tone: syllabus-close vs extra reasoning / multi-step items.
  • Hall experience is the same standard; the difference is paper design and timing.
  • Results format: centre bands vs scholar-style tiers / optional city percentile.
  • First-time olympiad vs students already used to competitive-style papers.

✕ What we don’t compare here

For these topics, the Exams listing or Contact page is a better fit.

  • “Which is tougher” vs IMO / NSO / SOF or other brands — this isn’t a public leaderboard.
  • Subject A vs Subject B (e.g. Math vs English) — subject choice happens at registration, not in this table.
  • School A vs School B ranking — we focus on centre-level reporting.
  • A live fee calculator — fees are confirmed at enrolment for your centre and sitting.

Which track should I choose?

These scenarios are a guide — the final decision is parent + teacher judgement + what suits the child.

Choose School Track if…

  • It’s their first hall-style sitting.
  • The class has mixed levels and you want everyone included.
  • You want simple band reports without percentile pressure.
  • Nursery–primary: more picture / oral support is needed.

Choose Scholar Track if…

  • They already work through competitive-style sets.
  • You want stretch plus a ranking narrative (where offered).
  • Class 6+ and they enjoy reasoning / multi-step items.
  • You’re consciously preparing for inter-school / zone events.

Questions & answers

Do the two tracks have different fees?

Exact fees depend on centre, track, and bundle. Scholar sometimes includes extra material or time — the amount due is stated on your registration confirmation or centre calendar.

Can we switch tracks within the same year?

Usually yes, on the next sitting or cycle. You can’t mix papers from both tracks in one sitting — one track per enrolment.

Does Scholar mean “better student”?

No. Both tracks are dignified. Scholar only names paper depth and reporting style — we don’t rank children as people.

Does this page book my papers?

No — it’s decision help only. Papers are issued after registration, per the schedule.

Where is the full subject list?

See Exams & programmes for grade-wise chips and bundles — subject lines are detailed there.
