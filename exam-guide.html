1. Overview

Every Qolympiad sitting follows the same backbone: register → receive confirmation & admit card → report to the assigned centre → sit papers under invigilation → receive bands/ranks as per programme rules. Exact dates, room numbers, and multi-paper schedules are always printed on your admit card and in the centre notice for your batch — this page explains the general pattern.

2. Step-by-step process

  1. Choose programme & papers — Pick School or Scholar track and subjects on the registration form, or follow your school’s bulk instructions.
  2. Pay & submit — Complete payment as per the instructions sent after you submit. Keep the transaction reference.
  3. Confirmation — You receive confirmation by email and/or SMS with centre name, city, and batch / wave (e.g. morning or afternoon slot where applicable).
  4. Admit card — Issued before the sitting (typically a few days to a week ahead, depending on centre). It carries roll number, venue address, reporting time, paper list, and special instructions (OMR, stationery, etc.).
  5. Exam day — Arrive at reporting time, complete verification, take assigned seat, listen to instructions, write papers in sequence with scheduled gaps.
  6. Collection & exit — Answer sheets / OMR are collected by invigilators; students leave in an orderly manner as instructed.
  7. Results — Published as per the cycle communicated on the admit card or centre notice: Results area and/or centre handout.

3. Roll number & seating

Roll number

  • Your roll number is unique for that centre + sitting + student. It is not the same as school admission number.
  • It is printed on the admit card and often on desk labels or the seating chart at the venue.
  • If you lose the admit card before the exam, contact the centre or official@qolympiad.com / phone +91 9413128045 immediately with registered mobile — we reissue where possible before gate closure.

Seating & batch size

  • Seats are assigned by roll order in rows; invigilators direct you to the exact row and seat.
  • How many students sit in one room depends on hall capacity and fire/safety norms at that venue. Typical ranges are on the order of 25–40 candidates per room, but your centre may use larger or smaller rooms — the total “how many rolls” for a city is simply everyone registered for that batch, split across however many rooms the centre opens.
  • Mixed grades may share a hall with clear zone separation, or sit in separate rooms — your admit card states your room / block if applicable.

4. Reporting & paper timings

Authoritative time is always the one on your admit card. The table below is a typical pattern for planning (add ~30 minutes early arrival).

Stage Arrival Briefing & settle School track (paper) Scholar track (6–10)
Nursery – UKG As on card ~10 min · pictures / oral One integrated block · ~30–40 min (no separate Scholar paper for tiny grades)
Class 1 – 5 As on card ~15 min ~40–55 min per paper Scholar bundle: same day extra depth or scheduled paper — see card
Class 6 – 8 As on card ~15 min ~60 min typical ~75 min (+15 vs School)
Class 9 – 10 As on card ~15 min ~75–90 min (incl. OMR where used) +15 min vs School on same blueprint

Multiple papers same day: There is usually a short gap (about 15–30 minutes) between papers for water and washroom — exact gap on admit card. Scholar students doing more than one deep paper may have a longer day; the card lists order and times.

5. Exam day — what happens

  1. Gate — Reach at reporting time. Late entry may be refused after the gate closes (printed on card).
  2. Verification — Admit card + school ID / parent ID as instructed. No admit card may mean no entry.
  3. Seating — Sit only on your assigned roll / seat. Bags and phones go where invigilators direct (usually switched off, not on desk).
  4. Instructions — Listen to timing, question paper handling, OMR bubbling if any, and extra-time rules.
  5. During paper — Raise hand for doubts on print quality only; no chatting. Bathroom breaks as per centre rule.
  6. Finish — Stop when told; hand in sheets in order; leave when dismissed row-wise.

What to carry: Admit card, pens (blue/black as specified), pencils, eraser, ruler where allowed; transparent water bottle if permitted. No smartwatches / phones on person unless a specific paper explicitly allows a device (rare). Geometry box only if the card says so for that paper.

6. Programme snapshots

These match the bundles on Exams & programmes. Fees and exact paper count are confirmed at registration.

Little Learners Pack (Nursery – UKG)

Picture cards, oral prompts, short attention-friendly tasks. Usually one sitting, no multi-hour day. Parent or escort policy is stated on the centre notice.

Primary Olympiad Bundle (Class 1–5)

Math, English, Science, GK / logic combinations as selected. Papers are shorter than middle school; may be same morning or split across two visits depending on bundle — see admit card.

Middle Mastery (Class 6–8)

Integrated subjects plus olympiad-style practice. Expect 60 min (School) or 75 min (Scholar) per major paper. Mocks may be scheduled on separate dates inside the annual plan.

Secondary Board-ready (Class 9–10)

Timed series, board-weighted mix, OMR optional per paper. Longer stamina day possible; reporting time may be earlier for double-paper days.

Scholar Stretch (Class 3–10)

Deeper items and optional city percentile where the centre participates. +15 minutes in Classes 6–10 vs School track on comparable papers. May share a hall schedule with other grades but always follow your own admit card times.

7. After the exam

  • Processing time varies by cycle — your admit card or centre WhatsApp/SMS gives the expected window.
  • Results show bands / ranks as per track; they are not board marks. See Terms for limitations.
  • Queries on score or re-check follow the centre’s declared window; contact Contact Us with roll number and centre name.

8. FAQ

How many students sit in one hall?
It depends on room size and safety norms. Many venues use roughly 25–40 candidates per room, but your admit card shows your batch, room/block, and reporting time.
How do I get my roll number?
After registration, the centre generates a unique roll for that sitting. It is printed on your admit card.
What if I am late?
Doors may close at the reporting time on the card. Plan to arrive about 30 minutes early.
Are calculators allowed?
Only if the admit card explicitly allows them for that paper. Otherwise use rough work on the question paper or sheet provided.
How many papers in one day?
Usually 1–3 depending on your bundle. The exact order and gaps are on the admit card.
